# Sonus Health

> Sonus Health is a veterinary heart screening platform. Veterinary clinics and pet owners use a smartphone to record heart sounds from dogs, cats, and horses. AI analysis returns heart rate, HRV, and murmur detection in seconds. Full reports are reviewed by board-certified veterinary cardiologists and returned within 48 hours.

Sonus Health is built by Decorte Future Industries (DFI). The platform is available as a mobile app for pet owners (iOS and Android) and a web dashboard for veterinary clinics.

## How it works

1. **Record** — Place any smartphone against the animal's chest for 30 seconds.
2. **AI Analysis** — Heart rate, heart rate variability, and murmurs are detected in seconds.
3. **Expert Review** — A diplomate veterinary cardiologist reviews the recording and returns a full report within 48 hours. Automated follow-up workflows notify owners and clinics.

## What's measured

- Heart rate (beat-by-beat)
- Heart rate variability (HRV)
- Heart murmurs (detection, timing, intensity — e.g. systolic/diastolic, grade)
- Arrhythmias (rhythm irregularities, ectopic beats, gallop rhythms)
- Stress and pain indicators
- Heart Health Score (composite 1–10 rating)
